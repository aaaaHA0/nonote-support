隐私政策

更新于 2026 年 11 月 8 日

「没有日记」把日记、随手记、想做的事和草稿保存在设备上。你可以不注册 NoNote 账号，也可以在不授权照片或定位的情况下手动写记录。

哪些内容会同步到 iCloud

iCloud 同步默认开启。你登录 Apple 账户、且 iCloud 同步可用时，记录、日记、想做的事、草稿、附上的地点和照片引用，以及部分外观设置，会通过 Apple CloudKit 同步到同一账户下的设备。数据存放在你账户的私有数据库中，开发者不会从这个数据库读取你的记录。照片预览文件、后台到访的原始日志和本机权限设置留在设备上。

在「设置 → iCloud 同步」可以关闭同步，重新打开 App 后生效。关闭后，设备上已有的记录仍会保留，已经同步到 iCloud 的内容也不会因此删除。系统备份和 iCloud 照片由你在系统设置中管理。

照片、定位和提醒

你可以选择要附上的照片、为记录添加地点，或开启本地提醒。你开启并授权对应的 NoNote Pro 功能后，App 可以读取照片的拍摄时间和地点来整理相册，也可以在后台记下你停留过的地点。App 会在设备上保存小预览，照片图库里的原图仍由系统管理。你可以在系统设置中撤回照片、定位或通知权限，也可以在 App 设置中关闭相关功能。

本地智能、地图和购买

符合条件时，App 用 Apple 的本地模型整理中文日记或识别想做的事。用于生成的记录不会发送给开发者或第三方模型服务。搜索地名、显示地图和解析地址时，Apple 地图可能会处理你输入的查询或相关坐标。订阅购买、恢复和状态由 Apple 处理。NoNote 不接收你的支付卡信息。

广告、分析与反馈

NoNote 没有广告、跨 App 跟踪或第三方行为分析。你发送支持邮件或通过 TestFlight 提交反馈时，开发者会用你的回复地址、消息和随附的诊断信息回复你，并排查问题。你在 GitHub Issues 上发布的报告和评论对公众可见。请勿在反馈中附上私人日记、照片或其他不需要提供的信息。

本网站

本网站由 GitHub Pages 托管。GitHub 出于安全目的记录访客的 IP 地址。详情见 GitHub 隐私声明。

保留、导出与删除

记录会保留到你删除为止。App 清理本机到访日志时，会移除超过 14 天的原始日志条目。在「设置 → 导出与删除」可以导出记录、清除内容，或进入「最近删除」恢复或永久删除单条记录。同步开启且连接可用时，同步范围内的删除会传到同一账户下的其他设备。已导出的文件、系统备份和图库原图，请到保存这些副本的位置分别管理。

如果你对隐私政策有疑问，或希望查询或删除你提交给开发者的信息，请发邮件到 nonote.support@enthalnet.com。

Privacy Policy

Updated November 8, 2026

NoNote stores your diary, notes, things to do, and drafts on your device. You can write notes without a NoNote account or access to your photos or location.

What syncs to iCloud

iCloud sync is on by default. If you are signed in to your Apple Account and sync is available, your entries, diaries, things to do, drafts, attached places and photo references, and some appearance settings sync to devices on the same account through Apple CloudKit. That data is kept in your account’s private database. The developer does not read your entries from it. Photo previews, the raw background visit log, and permissions stay on the device.

Turn sync off in Settings → iCloud Sync, then reopen the app for the change to take effect. Turning sync off leaves existing entries on the device and does not delete data already synced to iCloud. Manage system backups and iCloud Photos in Apple’s Settings app.

Photos, location, and reminders

You can select photos to attach, add a place to an entry, or turn on local reminders. If you enable the relevant NoNote Pro feature and grant permission, NoNote can read when and where photos were taken to organize them or record places you stop in the background. NoNote keeps small previews on your device. Your originals stay in the system photo library. You can revoke access to Photos, Location, and Notifications in Apple’s Settings app or turn off the related features in NoNote.

On-device intelligence, maps, and purchases

On supported devices, NoNote uses Apple’s on-device model to prepare Chinese diary summaries or identify things to do. Entries used for generation are not sent to the developer or to a third-party model service. Apple Maps may process your queries or relevant coordinates when you search for a place, view a map, or look up an address. Apple handles purchases, restores, and subscription status. NoNote does not receive your payment card information.

Ads, analytics, and feedback

NoNote has no ads, cross-app tracking, or third-party behavior analytics. When you email support or send TestFlight feedback, the developer uses your reply address, message, and any included diagnostics to respond and investigate. Reports and comments you post to GitHub Issues are public. Please leave private diary entries, photos, and other unnecessary information out of feedback.

This website

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention, export, and deletion

NoNote keeps entries until you delete them. When the app cleans up the local visit log, it removes raw log entries older than 14 days. Use Settings → Export & Delete to export or clear your content, or open Recently Deleted to restore or permanently delete individual entries. When sync is on and a connection is available, deletions for synced content are sent to other devices on the same account. Manage exported files, system backups, and photo originals separately wherever those copies are stored.

For privacy questions, or to request access to or deletion of information you sent to the developer, email nonote.support@enthalnet.com.
