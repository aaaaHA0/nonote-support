隐私政策

更新于 2026 年 11 月 9 日

日记、随手记、想做的事和草稿会保存在你的设备上。手动写记录无需注册 NoNote 账号，也不需要照片或定位权限。

哪些内容会同步到 iCloud

iCloud 同步默认开启。登录 Apple 账户后，只要同步开启且服务可用，NoNote 就会通过 Apple CloudKit 将记录、日记、想做的事、草稿、保存的地点和照片引用，以及部分外观设置同步到同一账户的设备。手动添加照片和自动整理相册时生成的预览也会同步。为了让另一台 iPhone 能接着整理，NoNote 还会同步负责自动记录的 iPhone 的随机标识、该设备开启的自动记录功能、最近活动时间和相册整理进度。这些数据存放在你账户的私有数据库中，开发者无法从中读取你的记录。原图由系统照片图库管理。原始到访日志和本机权限设置只留在设备上。

部分较早版本在同步记录时只同步照片引用，预览留在设备上。

在「设置 → iCloud 同步」可以关闭同步，重新打开 App 后生效。关闭后，本机已有的记录会保留，已同步到 iCloud 的内容也不会被删除。系统备份和 iCloud 照片由你在系统设置中管理。

照片、定位和提醒

你可以选照片、为记录添加地点或开启本地提醒。手动添加照片时，如果照片带有拍摄地点，App 可能读取该地点并随照片保存在记录中。

开启并授权相应的 NoNote Pro 功能后，App 可以按拍摄时间和地点整理相册，也可以在后台记录你停留过的地点。整理连拍时，记录里可能只显示一张代表照。开启「合并相似照片」后，App 还会在本机比较拍摄时间相近的照片缩略图，每组相似照片只显示一张代表照。其余照片仍在系统图库里。只要 App 还能访问这些照片，记录也可以编辑，你就可以把它们放回记录。在「设置 → 自动记录」可以关闭「合并相似照片」。

你可以在系统设置里撤回照片、定位或通知权限，也可以在 App 设置里关闭相关功能。

本地智能、地图和购买

在符合条件的设备上，NoNote 使用 Apple 的本地模型整理中文日记或识别想做的事。用于生成的内容不会发送给开发者或第三方模型服务。

搜索地点、显示地图或查询地名时，Apple 地图可能处理你输入的查询或相关坐标。购买、恢复购买和订阅状态由 Apple 处理，NoNote 不接收支付卡信息。

广告、分析与反馈

NoNote 没有广告、跨 App 跟踪或第三方行为分析。你发送支持邮件或通过 TestFlight 提交反馈时，开发者会用你提供的联系信息、消息和随附诊断信息来回复和排查问题。你在 GitHub Issues 发布的报告和评论对公众可见。发送反馈时，请不要附上与问题无关的私人记录或照片。

本网站

本网站由 GitHub Pages 托管。GitHub 出于安全目的记录访客的 IP 地址。详情见 GitHub 隐私声明。

保留、导出与删除

在「设置 → 导出与删除」可以导出或清除内容。单条记录移到「最近删除」后，可以恢复或永久删除。开启 iCloud 同步且连接可用时，同步范围内的删除会传到同一账户的其他设备。

保存的记录会保留到你删除为止。原始到访日志单独保存在本机。App 启动时会清理超过 14 天的日志条目。已导出的文件、系统备份和图库原图，请在保存这些副本的位置分别管理。

如果你对隐私政策有疑问，或想查询、删除你发给开发者的信息，请发邮件至 nonote.support@enthalnet.com。

Privacy Policy

Updated November 9, 2026

NoNote stores your diary, notes, things to do, and drafts on your device. You can write notes without a NoNote account or access to your photos or location.

What syncs to iCloud

iCloud sync is on by default. When you’re signed in to your Apple Account and sync is on and available, NoNote uses Apple CloudKit to sync entries, diaries, things to do, drafts, saved places, photo references, and some appearance settings across devices on that account. Previews of photos added manually or through automatic organizing sync as well. To let another iPhone pick up where it left off, NoNote also syncs a random identifier for the iPhone that records automatically, which automatic recording features are on, when it was last active, and how far it got with organizing photos. This data is stored in your account’s private database, which the developer cannot use to read your records. The system photo library manages your originals. Raw visit logs and device permissions stay on the device.

Some earlier versions sync photo references with your records and keep previews on the device.

To turn sync off, go to Settings → iCloud Sync and reopen the app. Existing records stay on the device, and data already synced remains in iCloud. Manage system backups and iCloud Photos in Apple’s Settings app.

Photos, location, and reminders

You can attach photos, add a place to an entry, or turn on local reminders. If a photo you attach contains location information, NoNote may read it and save it with the photo in your entry.

If you enable the relevant NoNote Pro feature and grant permission, NoNote can group library photos by when and where they were taken or record places where you stop in the background. For a burst, the entry may show just one representative shot. With Merge Similar Photos on, NoNote also compares thumbnails of photos taken around the same time on your device and shows one representative shot from each group of similar photos. The other photos stay in your system photo library. You can add them back if NoNote can still access them and the entry is editable. Turn off Merge Similar Photos in Settings → Auto records.

You can revoke access to Photos, Location, or Notifications in Apple’s Settings app or turn off the related features in NoNote.

On-device intelligence, maps, and purchases

On supported devices, NoNote uses Apple’s on-device model to prepare Chinese diary summaries or identify things to do. NoNote does not send the inputs for these features to the developer or to a third-party model service.

Apple Maps may process your queries or relevant coordinates when you search for a place, view a map, or look up a place name. Apple handles purchases, restores, and subscription status. NoNote does not receive payment card information.

Ads, analytics, and feedback

NoNote has no ads, cross-app tracking, or third-party behavioral analytics. If you email support or send TestFlight feedback, the developer uses the contact information, message, and any diagnostics you share to respond and investigate. Reports and comments you post on GitHub Issues are public. Please leave out private entries or photos that aren’t needed to explain the issue.

This website

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention, export, and deletion

Use Settings → Export & delete to export or clear your content. You can restore or permanently delete individual entries from Recently Deleted. With iCloud sync on and a connection available, deletions for synced content are sent to devices on the same account.

NoNote keeps saved entries until you delete them. It keeps the raw visit log separately on your device. When the app starts, it removes log entries older than 14 days. Manage exported files, system backups, and photo originals separately wherever those copies are stored.

For privacy questions, or to request access to or deletion of information you sent to the developer, email nonote.support@enthalnet.com.
