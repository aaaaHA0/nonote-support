技术支持

报告问题或提建议

在 GitHub Issues 公开报告问题或提建议。请附上 App 版本、系统版本和复现步骤。请勿提交日记、个人截图、联系方式或隐私请求。

管理订阅

在 App 内打开「设置 → NoNote Pro」可以恢复购买。要更改或取消订阅，请前往系统设置里的 Apple 账户订阅页面。

导出和同步

在「设置 → 导出与删除」可以管理记录。若同步中断，可到「设置 → iCloud 同步」查看状态。

Support

Report a problem or suggest a feature

Use GitHub Issues to post a public report. Include your app version, system version, and the steps to reproduce the problem. Keep diary entries, personal screenshots, contact details, and privacy requests out of public reports.

Manage a subscription

Open Settings → NoNote Pro in the app to restore purchases. To change or cancel a subscription, open Subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Export and sync

Use Settings → Export & Delete in NoNote to manage your records. If sync is interrupted, check Settings → iCloud Sync for its status.
